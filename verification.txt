EdgeLink 0.3.3 — audio continuity and local-output mute

Observed on the user's Mac: ScreenCaptureKit audio callbacks contain 960 frames at 48 kHz (20 ms). Previously Windows requested playback with a fixed 10 ms reserve and could start without enough data for the full driver request. The replacement accounts for source batch size and full output requests, adapts reserve after underruns (up to 60 ms), flushes stale tail and fades recovery transitions.
